What many students notice

Most community college students do more than go to school. Many work, care for children or parents, or both. A college schedule is built around classes, but your life is built around everything else too. Feeling that you have too much to do is common. It is not a sign that you do not belong in college.

  • College work happens outside class. Many colleges plan for about two hours of study each week for every hour in class. Check your syllabus for what your instructor expects.
  • Problems are easier to solve early. A schedule change at work is a small problem on Monday and a big one on the night an assignment is due.
  • Instructors expect life to happen. Many will work with a student who tells them about a problem before a deadline.

What helps

  1. Put every due date in one place. At the start of the term, copy all due dates from each syllabus into one calendar, on paper or on your phone.
  2. Plan your week, not just your day. On one day each week, look at the week ahead. Find the time you have for each class, and write it down.
  3. Use small pieces of time. Thirty minutes on a break can be enough to read a few pages or review notes.
  4. Tell people what you need. Tell your family when you need quiet time. Ask your manager about your schedule before the term starts.
  5. Contact your instructor early. If you see a problem coming, write before the due date, not after. See the example below.

Language focus: deadline words

Language focus

Small words with big meaning

Due dates use short words that change the meaning. Read them carefully.

Words used with deadlines
WordMeaningExample
byat that time or earlierSubmit the essay by Friday at 11:59 p.m.
untilcontinuing up to that timeThe quiz is open until Sunday.
beforeearlier than that timeRead Chapter 3 before class.
on / at / ina day / a clock time / a month or part of the dayon Monday, at 3 p.m., in March, in the morning
withinno later than the end of that timeI reply to email within two days.
Watch for this

Due by Friday means Friday is the last day. You may submit it earlier. Check the time too: 11:59 p.m. is one minute before midnight, still on Friday.
