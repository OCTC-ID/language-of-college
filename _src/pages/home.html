The Language of College

A Reading, Writing & Communication Toolkit

Short lessons for the reading, writing, and communication you do in college classes. Every page is free, and you do not need to sign in.

How to use this toolkit

  • Start on any page. Each page teaches one skill and makes sense without the others.
  • Learn, then try. Each page has an explanation, an example, and a short practice with answers.
  • Use your tools. You can translate any page with your browser. Example sentences stay in English so you can study them.

Read it offline

Download the whole toolkit to read without an internet connection. Each download has every finished page. The website always has the newest version.

  • Download the PDFBest for printing, or for reading on a computer.
  • Download the EPUBBest for a phone or tablet. Open it in a book app, such as Apple Books or Google Play Books, and make the text any size you like.
